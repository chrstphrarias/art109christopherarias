About Me

Hello, I am Christopher Arias, I am studying English and Digital Media Art at San Jose State University. I strive to involve myself in the publishing, media, and content creation industries of Silicon Valley where I can explore my storytelling and digital media skills to their fullest. I seek to create art that exudes an atmosphere of absurd humor, unique color, and warm comfort. A website that is straight to the point denies the user an experience; we should be inconvenienced for the sake of experiencing.
